MAJOR PROJECT

Campus Response System (CRS)

An AI-powered voice enquiry system that answers student and parent questions 24/7 in English and Malayalam.

Overview

Colleges receive repetitive enquiries about admissions, fees, courses, timings, and facilities. CRS automates these enquiries through a voice interface: callers ask a question and receive a spoken answer. Calls and queries are logged for staff review, and unresolved questions are escalated.

How it works

  1. Callers reach an Exotel IVR greeting: “Welcome to Campus Response System”.
  2. Speech is converted to text; Malayalam queries pass through translation to English.
  3. The query processor searches the college FAQ database.
  4. When no FAQ matches, the query is sent to the OpenAI model.
  5. A response is selected and converted to speech for playback through the IVR.
  6. The caller number, query, response, status, and timestamps are saved to the call log.
  7. Unresolved or unsatisfactory queries are escalated to staff for follow-up.

Key features

  • 24/7 voice-based enquiry handling in English and Malayalam.
  • FAQ knowledge base with AI fallback for unknown queries.
  • Automatic call logging and admin escalation.
  • A login-protected Command Center with system status, call volume, call logs, FAQ management, and a reply interface.
  • FAQ management in English and Malayalam, sorting newest calls first, and a light/dark theme toggle.
  • Architecture includes SMS and WhatsApp notifications alongside voice.

Technology and team

Tech stack: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Exotel Voice API, OpenAI, speech-to-text, gTTS / google-tts-api, Git/GitHub, and VS Code.

Team: Group 12, four students from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, College of Engineering Cherthala (APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University), guided by Ms. Janu R Panicker.

Database design

  • College FAQ: ID, query, answer, and language.
  • Call log: phone number, time, query, status, and response type.
  • Admin: dashboard login credentials.

Reported results

  • About 85% of queries were resolved automatically; about 15% went to admin review.
  • FAQ matching accuracy was reported at 90–95%.
  • Responses took 1–3 seconds when an FAQ was found; recording a new query for admin review took 2 seconds or less.
  • Average response time improved from about 2.8 seconds to 1.9 seconds during testing.

My role

  • Built Node.js backend endpoints for telephony webhooks and call events.
  • Integrated the voice API with the backend to handle incoming calls.
  • Designed the PostgreSQL schema for FAQs, call logs, and notification records, and wrote SQL queries to retrieve and update data.

System design and impact

Design artifacts included a layered architecture, Level 0–2 data flow diagrams, use-case, sequence and activity diagrams, an ER diagram, and a July 2025–March 2026 Gantt chart. The COCOMO estimate was about 3 KLOC, roughly 10 person-months, and about 6 months duration.

CRS reduces routine manual enquiries, provides consistent answers outside office hours, and gives administrators data about enquiry trends.

Limitations and future scope

The system depends on its FAQ database, internet access, and third-party voice services; it has limited multilingual support and can be affected by noise and accents. Planned improvements include stronger AI intent prediction, more languages, web and mobile integration, analytics visualisation, and stronger authentication and encryption.
